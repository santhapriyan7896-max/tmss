What happens when you walk in.

No sales pitch, no rush. Just honest advice from people who have done this every admission season for 15 years.

Book a session
  1. 01

    We listen first.

    Marks, interests, budget, what the family hopes for. One unhurried conversation with the student and parents together.

  2. 02

    We lay out the options.

    Every course that genuinely fits, including good ones most families have never heard of.

  3. 03

    We shortlist colleges.

    Based on cutoff, fees, distance from home and placement record, with honest pros and cons for each.

  4. 04

    We handle counselling and paperwork.

    TNEA, NEET, paramedical and agriculture counselling, choice filling, applications and documents, done with you.

  5. 05

    We help with the fees.

    Government scholarships, first-graduate benefits and education loans, so money doesn't decide the future.

  6. 06

    We stay till joining day.

    Our job isn't done at the shortlist. We follow up until the student is sitting in class.

Questions parents ask us.

When should we come to you?

The earlier the better. During 12th standard is ideal, so entrance exams, applications and counselling can be planned in time. Right after results is the latest you should leave it.

Which courses do you guide for?

Every major stream after 12th: Engineering, Medical and AYUSH, Nursing, Allied Health, Pharmacy, Agriculture, Veterinary, Arts and Science, Management, Law, Architecture, Hotel Management, Aviation, PG courses and Study Abroad.

Do you help with TNEA and NEET counselling?

Yes. Registration, choice filling and every round of counselling. We help the student pick the best options for their rank.

We're not from Karur. Can you still help?

Yes. Talk to us on phone or WhatsApp first, and visit the office when it suits you.

Is the first session free?

Yes. The first conversation, to understand the student and their options, is free.
